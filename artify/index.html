// About the Studio

Precision is
our language.

Headquartered in Bathinda, Punjab, Artify Studios is an independent graphic design practice directed by Mukul Chhabra. We apply the logical grids, objective styling, and heavy typographic focus of the Swiss International Style to regional brand identities, print media catalogs, and high-impact commercial packaging solutions.

Every project starts with structure. We believe that design without a grid is noise, and identity without consistency is forgettable. Our work exists at the intersection of editorial rigor and commercial impact.
